## Nome
Degenscan Intel — market data for trading agents (Hyperliquid, options, on-chain, AI models)

## Descrição curta (≤ 160 caracteres)
Pay-per-call market data for AI agents: Hyperliquid funding, liquidations, smart money, options, token risk, gas, LLM prices. x402 USDC, from $0.001.

## Descrição longa
61 MCP tools over primary sources: Hyperliquid funding on every dex (incl. HIP-3), liquidation maps, smart-money positioning, Deribit options (IV, skew, max pain), token contract risk (honeypot, taxes, owner powers), Base gas and balances, Polymarket odds and leaderboard, US Treasury auctions, stablecoin supply and yields, SEC filings, and AI Intel (LLM prices per 1M tokens, cheapest model for a need, framework adoption, AI regulation dates). 100 free calls/day; then USDC per call via x402 (Base or Solana) or an API key. Information and analytics only — not investment advice.
